What is Sprint execution?

Sprint execution is the day-to-day work the Developers perform to achieve the Sprint Goal. After Sprint Planning sets the goal and selects Product Backlog items, the team turns those items into a tangible increment through design, coding, testing, and integration. Work is made transparent on a Sprint Backlog, and progress is inspected frequently so the team can adapt its plan as new information emerges.

Execution is guided by collaboration and fast feedback. The team holds a short Daily Scrum to synchronize, surface impediments, and adjust the plan for the next 24 hours. Quality is built in: done work meets the Definition of Done and is integrated into a potentially shippable increment. If a task proves larger than expected, the team re-plans while protecting the Sprint Goal. Stakeholders are engaged through reviews and timely clarification so the increment reflects real customer needs.

Example: Suppose the Sprint Goal is “Improve product search relevance.” During execution the team implements ranking tweaks, adds tests, analyzes logs, and pairs with a data analyst to verify results. Each day they update the Sprint Backlog and adjust tasks. By Sprint end, the increment is integrated, tested, and demo-ready.
